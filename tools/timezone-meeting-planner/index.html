Timezone Meeting Planner

Find a time that works across cities, with date-aware daylight-saving offsets.

1. Timezones & working hours

Up to 6 timezones. Hours are local to each city.

Type a city in an IANA name, e.g. America/New_York, Europe/London, Asia/Kathmandu, or UTC.

Working hours must start and end on the same local day. Weekdays means Monday–Friday; holidays are not considered.

2. Pick a day & duration

Start times cover 00:00–23:30 UTC on this date, every 30 minutes. Local dates may be the previous or next day. A slot fits only if the entire meeting is within everyone's working hours.

3. Compare times

No shared working-hour slots on this UTC day. Try a different day, duration, or working hours.
